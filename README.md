# Retail Sales Performance — Excel + Power BI Portfolio Project

## Executive Summary
A practical business analytics project demonstrating how transactional retail data can be prepared, analyzed, and communicated through Excel and Power BI.

## Objective
Identify revenue, profitability, product, regional, and channel performance patterns that can support commercial decisions.

## Analytics Workflow
**Data → Validation → Excel Analysis → KPI Design → Power BI Reporting → Business Insights**

## Excel Scope
- Structured sales dataset
- KPI calculations
- Product-level revenue and profit analysis
- Regional performance analysis
- Workbook prepared for Power BI ingestion

## Power BI Dashboard Scope
- Revenue KPI
- Profit KPI
- Profit Margin KPI
- Units Sold KPI
- Monthly Revenue & Profit trend
- Revenue and Profit by Product
- Revenue and Profit by Region
- Profit Margin by Channel
- Interactive slicers for Date, Region, Product, and Channel

## Recommended Data Model
Single sales fact table with dimensions represented by Product, Region, Channel, and Date fields.
